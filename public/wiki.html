2. The KNEC Monopoly Myth: Legal Jurisdiction in Kenya

A common misconception during evaluations is that the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC) already possesses a centralized verification portal for all academic records. In reality, Kenya's educational governance is strictly bifurcated by statute:

Evaluation Dimension KNEC QMIS (`qmis.knec.ac.ke`) ChetiCheck Higher-Ed Platform
Legal Mandate Basic Education: Primary & Secondary (KCPE / KCSE) Autonomous University Charters & CUE Regulation
Verification Latency 10 to 14 working days Under 200 milliseconds (Instant)
Verification Cost KES 2,280 – KES 2,320 per query letter Free for graduate (KES 250 for corporate API query)
Offline Capability Zero (Requires portal access + courier delivery) 100% Offline Capable via mobile QR verification
Tamper Proofing Physical stamped paper letters (vulnerable to forgery) Mathematical Ed25519 digital signatures

Legal note: Under the Universities Act (No. 42 of 2012), university degrees are legally granted under independent institutional charters. KNEC has zero legal authority or database records regarding university degrees.

3. Centralized Database Honeypots vs Asymmetric Cryptography

Why Traditional Databases Fail Against Insider Threats

In a traditional university system, degree information sits in a standard SQL database. If a malicious insider, rogue registrar staff, or database administrator modifies a student's graduation classification from "Pass" to "First Class Honours", the centralized database will report that forged record as valid to anyone who queries it.

The ChetiCheck Cryptographic Solution:

In ChetiCheck, degree authenticity is sealed by the university's private signing key at graduation time. Even if an attacker gains full administrative access to our database and changes the degree classification, the mathematical hash of the record will no longer match the university's digital signature. The verification engine immediately flags the altered credential with a bold red warning.

4. Why Not Blockchain?

  • Zero Gas Fees: Issuing thousands of certificates on Ethereum or Polygon costs significant transaction fees. ChetiCheck issuance cost is 0 KES.
  • KDPA 2019 Privacy Compliance: Public blockchains are permanently immutable. Under Section 35 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 (Right to Erasure), permanent on-chain personal data creates severe regulatory liability. ChetiCheck uses cryptographic shredding to ensure full statutory compliance.
  • Microsecond Performance: Asymmetric cryptographic verification executes in under 2 milliseconds on any browser without blockchain block confirmation delays.
