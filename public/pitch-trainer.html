High Severity Defense #1: The KNEC Objection
"The Kenyan government already has KNEC and a national database. Why would universities need ChetiCheck?"
KNEC has zero jurisdiction, zero database records, and zero legal mandate over university degrees. Under the Universities Act 2012, Kenyan chartered universities are legally autonomous degree-granting bodies regulated by CUE, not KNEC. KNEC QMIS takes 14 days and costs KES 2,300 for basic school certificates. ChetiCheck solves the multi-billion-shilling university degree verification vacuum in 200 milliseconds.
Punchline: "KNEC verifies KCPE and KCSE. It does not touch university degrees. ChetiCheck protects higher education."
Architecture Defense #2: The Blockchain Trap
"Why didn't you put these credentials on Ethereum or Polygon?"
Blockchains impose gas fees ($2 to $15 per issuance) which Kenyan universities will not pay for 5,000 graduates. Public blockchains also violate Section 35 of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 (Right to Erasure) because on-chain data cannot be erased. Asymmetric Ed25519 cryptographic signatures provide identical mathematical non-repudiation with 0 gas cost and instant offline verification.
Punchline: "We use mathematical law, not speculative tokens with gas fees and data protection liabilities."
Security Defense #3: Rogue Registrar Database Edits
"What happens if a corrupt database administrator edits student records in Neon PostgreSQL?"
In a centralized system, a database edit successfully forges a degree. In ChetiCheck, the verification engine recomputes the SHA-256 hash of the payload and tests the university's private Ed25519 signature. Because the corrupt admin does not have the university's HSM private key, any edited grade immediately fails signature validation.
Punchline: "A rogue admin can change rows in PostgreSQL, but they cannot forge asymmetric mathematics."
Business Model Defense #4: University Procurement Delays
"Kenyan university procurement under PPADA 2015 takes 18 months. How do you ever close deals?"
We do not sell enterprise IT overhaul tenders to university councils. Issuance is free for faculty and graduates. We monetize the corporate recruitment demand side: corporate banks, recruitment agencies, and government HR departments pay KES 250 per instant API query to replace 3-week manual registry clearance processes.
Punchline: "Free for universities and students. Monetized on corporate employer HR verification."
